eventReport: |
  The Deaf Community Advocacy Forum brought together members of the Deaf community,
  educators, advocates and accessibility leaders in Nairobi for an afternoon of
  meaningful conversation and shared experiences.

  Discussions focused on inclusion, representation and equal access, with participants
  sharing real experiences of navigating education, public services and other spaces
  where accessibility remains a challenge.

  The forum also created an opportunity for different stakeholders to connect,
  exchange ideas and identify practical ways of strengthening advocacy for Deaf
  people in Kenya.

  The event concluded with a shared commitment to continue creating spaces where
  Deaf voices are heard, represented and included in decisions that affect their lives.
