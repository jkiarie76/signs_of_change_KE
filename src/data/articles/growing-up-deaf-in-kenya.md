# Growing Up Deaf in Kenya

Growing up Deaf in Kenya can mean navigating a world that does not always understand Deafness while building strong identities, friendships, and communities.

## Finding community

For many Deaf young people, meeting others who share their language and experiences can be transformative. Schools, families, Deaf organisations, and community spaces can all provide opportunities to connect.

## Education and opportunity

Access to education is about more than simply being present in a classroom. Communication access, qualified interpreters, appropriate learning materials, and teachers who understand Deaf learners all matter.

## Building confidence

Deaf young people should have opportunities to develop their talents, express themselves, and participate fully in their communities.

## Looking forward

A more inclusive Kenya is one where Deaf young people can access education, employment, technology, culture, and public life without unnecessary barriers.
