## About the Campaign

Access to healthcare should not depend on whether a Deaf patient happens to have someone available to interpret for them.

The **KSL Integration in Public Healthcare Systems** campaign focuses on improving communication accessibility for Deaf people seeking healthcare in Kenya.

The campaign advocates for reliable access to certified Kenyan Sign Language interpreters within level 4 and 5 public referral hospitals.

## Why This Matters

Healthcare conversations can involve sensitive and highly personal information.

Patients need to be able to explain their symptoms, understand medical information, ask questions and make informed decisions about their treatment.

When communication barriers exist, Deaf patients may struggle to receive the same level of understanding, privacy and participation as other patients.

Professional Kenyan Sign Language interpretation can help create a more accessible healthcare environment.

## The Current Situation

Access to Kenyan Sign Language interpretation is not consistent across healthcare facilities.

In situations where an interpreter is unavailable, Deaf patients may depend on relatives, friends or other people to help communicate with healthcare professionals.

This can create challenges around accuracy, independence and privacy.

The campaign therefore focuses on encouraging healthcare institutions to develop sustainable systems for communication accessibility.

## What We Are Working Toward

The campaign is working toward:

- Greater availability of certified Kenyan Sign Language interpreters.
- Clear accessibility procedures within public hospitals.
- Better awareness of Deaf patients' communication needs.
- More inclusive healthcare services.
- Stronger recognition of communication accessibility as part of quality healthcare.
