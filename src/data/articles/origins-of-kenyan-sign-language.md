# The Origins of Kenyan Sign Language

Kenyan Sign Language has a rich history shaped by Deaf communities, schools, families, interpreters, and advocates across Kenya.

## Early development

The development of Kenyan Sign Language is closely connected to Deaf education and the communities that grew around it.

## A living language

Kenyan Sign Language is more than a collection of hand signs. It is a complete language used by Deaf people to communicate, express identity, share culture, and build community.

> Deaf people should be able to tell their own stories in their own language.

## Why its history matters

Understanding the history of Kenyan Sign Language helps us understand Deaf culture and the importance of language access today.
