# Technology and the Future of Sign Language Access

Technology is creating new opportunities for people to learn, practise, and access sign language.

## Digital learning

Websites, mobile applications, and video platforms can make sign language resources available to people wherever they are.

## Visual-first experiences

Sign language is visual, so digital platforms can provide learning experiences that prioritise video, clear demonstrations, captions, and accessible navigation.

## Building better tools

Technology should support Deaf communities rather than make assumptions about them. The best tools are developed with Deaf people involved in the design and testing process.

## Looking ahead

As digital technology continues to develop, there is an opportunity to create more accessible ways for people to learn Kenyan Sign Language and communicate across communities.
