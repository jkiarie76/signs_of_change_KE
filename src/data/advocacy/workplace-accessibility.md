## About This Resource

Deaf employees should be able to participate fully in the workplace and have access to reasonable communication and accessibility support.

This resource provides an introduction to workplace accessibility, employer responsibilities and practical considerations for creating more inclusive working environments.

## Understanding Workplace Accessibility

Accessible workplaces can include appropriate communication support, accessible information, inclusive policies and reasonable accommodations.

## Why It Matters

When workplaces remove unnecessary communication and accessibility barriers, Deaf employees are better positioned to contribute, grow professionally and participate equally.

## Practical Areas To Consider

- Accessible communication
- Workplace accommodations
- Inclusive meetings
- Accessible training materials
- Communication support
- Awareness among colleagues and managers
