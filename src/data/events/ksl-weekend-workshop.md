## About the Event

The Kenyan Sign Language Weekend Workshop brings together Deaf community members, interpreters, educators, students and anyone interested in strengthening their understanding and use of Kenyan Sign Language.

The workshop combines practical communication exercises with discussions around accessibility, Deaf culture and everyday communication.

## What You'll Learn

Participants will explore:

- Everyday Kenyan Sign Language communication
- Practical communication strategies
- Deaf-friendly communication environments
- The role of interpreters in inclusive spaces
- Common accessibility challenges
- Ways hearing people can communicate more effectively with Deaf people

## What to Expect

The workshop will include practical demonstrations, interactive exercises, group discussions and opportunities to practice Kenyan Sign Language in a supportive environment.

Participants are encouraged to ask questions and engage directly with Deaf community members and interpreters.

## Accessibility

Kenyan Sign Language interpretation will be available throughout the workshop.

The venue is wheelchair accessible, and printed materials will be provided in accessible formats.
