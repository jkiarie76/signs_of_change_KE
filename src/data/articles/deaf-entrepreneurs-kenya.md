Community has always played an important role in Deaf life.

Across Kenya, Deaf people continue to create spaces where they can communicate freely, build friendships and share experiences without having to constantly adapt to a hearing environment.

These spaces can take many forms. They may be community organizations, social gatherings, sporting activities, churches, schools or informal networks created by Deaf people themselves.

What connects them is the opportunity to communicate naturally and develop relationships through shared language and experience.

For many Deaf people, these communities provide something that accessibility alone cannot provide: belonging.

As Deaf communities continue to grow and connect across Kenya, these spaces remain an important part of Deaf culture and identity.
